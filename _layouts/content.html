Current Project -

The current project I am working on is a team project where we are collaborating to create a game called “Feral”, this is a platformer-style beat ''em up where you are a hybrid animal escaping a facility, defeating waves of guards, platforming over obstacles, and solving small puzzles. My role on the game development team is to build and design the levels, including where waves will spawn, key areas of interest, and lighting and particles.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/Screenshot 2026-04-28 185238.png" %}
{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/Screenshot 2026-04-28 185058.png" %}

In Feral, I have taken on the role of working on level design/artwork, animation and coding some small elements in the game, such as the eye, which tracks the player's movements. I have placed the eye to look slightly behind, as this gave it a better effect. This was coded in C#.
Within the game level design and working on the lighting and particle effects in the levels, the artwork was created using asprite which is styled for 64x64 for tile mapping as to add more detail/ texture to the levels and is one of my favourite tools for creating my artwork. I have tried to add depth using perspective floor and depth into the furniture/object player will encounter in the game, and adding foreground items for the player to walk behind

Personal Works -

In my free time, I have been experimenting with elements for a game, from world-building to art assets. Currently, I am exploring depth and layer heights within game levels, taking inspiration from games like Hollow Knight and Rain World for how they build depth into their worlds.

Additionally, using a more unique art approach of doing my drawing in a low pixel count of 20x20 per tile, as I continue to build this world, it will allow me to do so easily and still be quite effective, as I am planning to use a static camera to help not make it look as pixelated.
